# Changelog

All notable changes to FocusGrove are documented here.

## [2.0.0] — 2026-11-21
### Added
- Timer & stopwatch focus modes (10–120 min presets or custom), 12 tree species growing through 5 stages, wither on give-up.
- Deep focus & strict mode, ambient sounds generated in-app (rain, ocean, birds, café, white noise), tags, planting phrases, post-session diary and break timer.
- Grove views (day/week/month/year/all), 4 scenes, shop unlocking species/scenes with earned sunlight points — never money.
- Full stats, streaks, 7-day & 30-day charts, breakdowns by tag/species, searchable timeline, 14 achievements.
- Plant Together rooms, friends & local leaderboard, notifications, dark mode, JSON/CSV export & import.
- Installable PWA, Android APK build (GitHub Actions) and iOS build workflow; GitHub Pages site.
