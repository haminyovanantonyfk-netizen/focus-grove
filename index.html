        Install — free forever, every platform

        Android: Chrome → ⋮ → “Install app”, or download the APK from this project's GitHub Releases.

        iPhone / iPad: Safari → Share → “Add to Home Screen” — full-screen, offline, like a native app.

        Real trees: Forest plants real trees through a paid partner programme. FocusGrove is free with no revenue, so it can't fund that — instead your grove tracks a personal pledge: every 100 virtual trees = plant / sponsor 1 real tree yourself, and we show your pledge count in Stats when you grow.

        FocusGrove is free & open source (MIT). An original app inspired by the plant-a-tree focus idea — not affiliated with Forest. All features free, no ads, no subscriptions.
